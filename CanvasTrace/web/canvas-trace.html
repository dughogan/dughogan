Put the reference on the canvas, not beside it.

Pick the image you want to transfer, aim your iPhone at the canvas, and pin the image to it. It stays glued to the canvas as you move, so you can trace edges and block in values freehand.

A blank canvas gives the tracker nothing to hold on to. Stick four small pieces of tape near the corners, or leave the easel and wall in view. Anything with edges on the canvas plane helps.
