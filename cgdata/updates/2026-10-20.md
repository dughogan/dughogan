  - **The room:** loft rooms with open window bays (the sun stays the key),
    wood floors, beams and a door.
  - **Furniture:** each piece is its own object (per-piece mattes): shelving
    with books, sofas, tables, chairs, real floor lamps, rugs, plants and
    pictures.
  - **Lighting:** fill and rim lights sit inside the room.
- **Hero kit v1** (`kits/heroes.py`): car, robot arm, torus knot, crystal
  cluster and twisted column, with clearcoat car paint, rubber and chrome.
  Captions name the hero's actual color.
- **Character kit v1** (`kits/character.py`): a rigged figure, mannequin-like
  in photoreal and big-headed in toon.
  - **Walk or idle:** the walk is at natural cadence with no foot sliding.
    The camera follows, and focus follows too.
  - **New pose-control data:** joint tracks are recorded and exported as an
    OpenPose-style **pose video**, with a `pose` pair set.
- **FX v1** (`kits/fx.py`): rigid-body destruction. A brick wall, crate stack
  or drum column beside the hero is knocked down by a projectile. It appears
  in 1 in 3 outdoor shots and never indoors.
- **Weathered materials:** grime rising from the ground, edge wear and
  roughness variation, with the AOVs carrying the true values.
- **Toon look:**
  - three-tone cel shading;
  - **ink lines computed from the data passes** (`lines.py`: object
    outlines, creases, silhouettes);
  - toon targets are inked, and a **`lineart` pair set** is exported.
- **Pilot package** (`package.py`): a buyer-ready folder.
  - A stratified sample of clips, with style pairs kept together.
  - Pair manifests that never point at a missing clip.
  - A generated **DATASHEET**, which checks the LTX format rather than
    asserting it.
  - A **PITCH_DRAFT** with the numbers filled in, plus each clip's
    provenance record.
- **Fixes:**
  - **Renders are now sharp, with DOF added in comp.** In-camera DOF was
    blurring twice once Nuke's ZDefocus ran, and made the depth, ID and
    normal passes noisy in defocused areas.
  - **Lights are hidden from the camera**, and the random floating practical
    lights now sit in visible lanterns.
  - **The rim light is a sized source**, and matte toon surfaces stay matte.
    Both were producing hot-spot "blobs".
  - **Props and lanterns are kept off the camera's sightline.**
