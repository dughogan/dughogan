## Done

- **Keying module v1** (`blender/kits/stage.py`): green/blue-screen clips
  for training keyers (CorridorKey / alpha-gen territory).
  - **The stage:** a cyclorama sized from the actual camera move, so the
    screen fills every frame.
    - Uneven screen lighting, optional tracking markers, and real shadows
      on the floor.
    - Studio key, fill and rim lights; no props, fog or FX.
  - **Exact ground truth per frame** (extra Cycles view layers):
    - the plate;
    - the foreground with spill;
    - the foreground without spill;
    - an exact alpha (fractional through glass and car windows);
    - the clean plate;
    - a shadow matte;
    - the screen's own light group.
  - **Export:** six new videos and five pair sets: `key_alpha`, `key_fg`,
    `key_clean`, `key_shadow` (each conditioned on the plate) and
    `key_despill`.
  - **How to render:** `run_batch --environment greenscreen`. Clips get
    their own `KY_` ids and are never drawn at random.
  - **Cost:** about four renders per frame.
- **Lens flare renderer v1** (`flares.py`, `run_batch --flares`): a flare
  layer for photoreal clips, added after rendering.
  - **The render is untouched:** clean = render + sun disc, flared = clean
    + flare.
  - **Diffraction starburst:** computed from the aperture at seven
    wavelengths, so blade count sets the spikes (6 or 14 in the samples)
    and they fringe spectrally.
  - **Ghosts:** aperture-shaped, with coating tints.
  - **Veiling glare:** included.
  - **The sun:** placed where Blender's ray cast says it is visible, and it
    flares even from just outside the frame.
  - **Consistent over time:** the lens is seeded per clip and sources come
    from each frame, so flares track their sources across frames.
  - **Export:** `flare_removal` and `flare_addition` pair sets.
  - **Cost:** about 2 s per pilot frame on CPU.
  - **Limitation:** this is a parametric lens model, not a ray trace of real
    lens designs. The datasheet says so automatically.
